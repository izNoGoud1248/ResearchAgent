---
name: literatureReview
title: Literature review
description: For in-depth or survey requests that ask for an overview of a field, its history or the state of the art.
---

Write a literature review of at most 900 words.

- Open with a `## Overview` of 3 to 5 sentences: what the field is about and where it stands now.
- Add `## Key papers`: a Markdown table with columns Year, Paper, Contribution, one row per important paper found, oldest first. The Paper cell is the citation, such as `[arXiv:2302.01318v2]`.
- Then 2 to 4 `##` sections, one per research direction, explaining how the approaches relate and how the field moved.
- Add `## Open problems`: 3 to 5 bullets, each tied to a paper that raises it where possible.
- End with `## Limitations` (what this review could not cover, for example papers outside arXiv), then `## References`.

## Checklist

- Has an overview of the field.
- Has a Key papers table with year, paper and contribution.
- Sections follow research directions, not individual papers.
- Lists open problems.
- States what the review could not cover.
